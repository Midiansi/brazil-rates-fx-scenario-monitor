## What can overturn the view

- Rates favor the dollar: U.S. two-year yields are higher and the saved meeting path narrows Brazil's rate advantage.
- Oil favors Brazil through export income, but the same supply shock can lift global inflation, yields and risk aversion.
- USD/BRL near its recent low says the dollar case is not winning yet; wait for price to resolve the conflict.

## One conditional paper trade

### Buy USD / sell BRL after confirmation - conditional, no current position

**Thesis:** Rates and oil point in opposite directions. Higher US yields and a potentially smaller Brazil-US rate gap favor the dollar, while oil above USD 100 can support Brazil's export income and the real. USD/BRL near its recent low shows that the dollar case is not yet winning, so the trade requires a confirmed break above 5.22.

**Entry trigger:** Wait for USD/BRL to close above 5.22, the top of its recent range. Until then, there is no trade.

**Latest PTAX reference:** 5.0915 BRL per USD on 11 Sep 2026.

**Invalidation:** Before entry, abandon the dollar thesis below 5.08 if oil stays firm. After entry, leave after two closes below 5.15, or if Brazil's rate advantage does not shrink.

**Review/profit-taking zone:** Reassess around 5.36-5.37, one recent-range width above entry. Technical basis: 5.2233 + 0.1380 = 5.3613.

**Expected holding period:** Approximately two to four weeks, spanning the 15-16 September decisions and initial post-meeting repricing.

**Supporting scenario:** Base case

**Three principal risks**

1. Oil and export income continue to support the real.
2. Brazil keeps its rate advantage or US yields fall.
3. Brazilian fiscal or election news attracts capital.

## Bottom line

Rates favor the dollar; oil and export income favor the real. Enter only after USD/BRL closes above 5.22 while the U.S. two-year yield stays near or above 4.56%. Until then, there is no trade. A move below 5.08 with firm oil rejects the idea before entry; after entry, leave after two closes below 5.15 or if the rate gap does not narrow.
