### Long USD / short BRL - conditional, no current position

**Thesis:** Enter only on a confirmed upside break in USD/BRL because the exchange-aligned base case narrows the Brazil-US policy differential while recent PTAX, Focus Selic and US 2-year moves already lean in the same direction.

**Entry trigger:** A daily PTAX midpoint closes above 5.22, the rounded saved 20-observation high of 5.2233, with US 2Y near or above 4.34% or no widening in the policy differential.

**Latest PTAX reference:** 5.1567 BRL per USD on 01 Sep 2026.

**Invalidation:** After entry, two consecutive PTAX midpoints below 5.16, or a differential still near/above 10.375 pp because Copom does not ease and the FOMC does not hike.

**Review/profit-taking zone:** 5.35-5.36; saved high 5.2233 + range width 0.1328 = 5.3561, bracketed to adjacent cents.

**Expected holding period:** Approximately two to four weeks, spanning the 15-16 September decisions and initial post-meeting repricing.

**Supporting scenario:** Base case

**Three principal risks**

1. Copom holds or gives materially hawkish guidance.
2. The FOMC holds or guides dovishly, pulling US 2-year yields and USD lower.
3. Risk-on sentiment, stronger commodities or favorable Brazilian fiscal news supports BRL.

## Bottom line

The trade activates only after a daily PTAX midpoint closes above 5.22 with US 2Y near/above 4.34% or no widening in the Brazil-US policy differential. Abandon the view on two post-entry PTAX midpoints below 5.16, a failure of the differential to narrow, materially lower US 2Y, or the hawkish relative to expectations outcome.
