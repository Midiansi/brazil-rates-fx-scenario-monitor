# Rates versus oil: September 2026 decision case

**Thesis updated:** 13 September 2026, 16:58 Brasília time
**Status:** Conditional educational paper trade; no position and no claimed performance.

## The idea in one sentence

Higher U.S. yields and a potentially smaller Brazil-U.S. rate gap favor the dollar, while oil above USD 100 supports Brazilian export income and the real; because USD/BRL is still near its recent low, I would wait for price to reveal which force is stronger.

## What the market is saying

- The U.S. two-year yield rose 0.31 percentage point over one month to **4.56%**, which supports the dollar.
- USD/BRL nevertheless fell 0.72% over one month to **5.0915**, close to the low of its latest 20-observation range.
- Brent reached **USD 109.51 per barrel** in the latest official observation. For Brazil this is two-sided: higher oil can increase export income and support the real, but it can also raise inflation, global yields and risk aversion.
- The 2026 Focus medians remain **13.75% for Selic** and **5.00% for inflation**. The current Brazil-U.S. policy-rate gap is **10.375 percentage points**.

## Why this is not a simple rates trade

The saved central-bank path—a 0.25-point Brazilian cut and a 0.25-point U.S. increase—would reduce Brazil's rate advantage to about 9.88 points. That normally argues for a weaker real. But the real has strengthened while U.S. yields rose, so another force is offsetting the rate signal. Oil and export flows provide a plausible explanation.

The useful signal is therefore the disagreement itself. I do not need to guess whether rates or oil will win; I can define what price would need to do before taking risk.

## Three meeting paths

| Scenario | Brazil | United States | Rate gap | What should confirm it |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Brazil stays tougher | Hold at 14.00% | Hold at 3.50%-3.75% | Near 10.38 pp | USD/BRL below 5.08 and lower U.S. two-year yields |
| Saved base case | Cut to 13.75% | Raise to 3.75%-4.00% | Near 9.88 pp | USD/BRL above 5.22 with the U.S. two-year near 4.56% |
| Brazil cuts faster | Cut to 13.50% | Raise to 3.75%-4.00% | Near 9.63 pp | USD/BRL above 5.22 with the U.S. two-year above 4.56% |

The B3 and CME meeting-pricing observations were saved on 1 September and are now dated anchors, not live probabilities. No joint probability is assigned.

## Conditional paper trade

**Direction:** Buy USD / sell BRL only after confirmation.

**Entry:** Wait for a daily PTAX midpoint above **5.22**, the rounded high of the refreshed 20-observation range, with the U.S. two-year yield near or above 4.56% or the policy-rate gap actually narrowing.

**Before-entry rejection:** If USD/BRL closes below **5.08** while oil stays firm, the commodity/export signal is winning and there is no dollar trade.

**After-entry invalidation:** Exit after two consecutive daily PTAX midpoints below **5.15**, or if Brazil's rate advantage does not narrow and export flows continue to support the real.

**Review zone:** Reassess around **5.36-5.37**, one refreshed range width above the range high. This is not a guaranteed target.

**Catalysts:** The 16 September Copom and FOMC decisions and guidance, plus changes in the Middle East oil-supply shock.

**Principal risks:** Oil and export income continue to support the real; Brazil keeps its rate advantage; U.S. yields fall; or Brazilian fiscal and election news attracts capital.
